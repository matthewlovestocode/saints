# Orthodox Saints

This application is meant to document the lives, teachings, feast days, and historical context of Orthodox saints.

The first saint documented by the application will be Moses the Black, also known as Abba Moses the Ethiopian. His life offers a powerful starting point for the project because of his transformation from violence and theft to repentance, monastic holiness, humility, and spiritual wisdom.

Initial documentation for each saint should include:

- Name and commonly used titles
- Feast day
- Historical period and location
- Short biography
- Major themes from the saint's life
- Sayings, writings, or teachings where available
- References and source notes

The goal is to build a respectful, well-sourced resource that helps readers learn about the saints of the Orthodox Church.
